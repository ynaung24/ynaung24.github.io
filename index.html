About Me

Hello! I'm Yan, an AI Engineer and Data Scientist passionate about building intelligent systems that solve real-world problems. Currently, I'm developing a Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) system that handles over 1000 daily queries, combining the power of large language models with robust information retrieval.

My work focuses on the intersection of machine learning, natural language processing, and scalable system design. I'm particularly excited about projects involving Large Language Models, Generative AI, and building production-ready ML infrastructure that can serve millions of users.

When I'm not working, I explore new ideas through side projects. Here are a few I'm currently building:

  • ▸ Developing a multi-modal emotion detection system for interview analysis using CNNs, RNNs, and LLMs, with an interactive dashboard for visualizing sentiment patterns and behavioral insights.
  • ▸ Building a neural style transfer platform that blends artistic movements with user photographs, leveraging convolutional neural networks to create unique artistic renditions.
  • ▸ Creating a personalized AI chatbot that mimics individual communication styles by training on user-specific corpora, powered by RAG for contextually aware responses.

I'm always eager to collaborate on challenging problems at the intersection of AI and engineering. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to discuss ideas or explore potential opportunities!
