About Me

Hello! I'm Yan, an AI Engineer and Data Scientist based in San Francisco. I finished my M.S. in Data Science and Engineering at the University of San Francisco in December 2025, after an M.S. in Applied Economics there. I'm now a Research Assistant at USF, working with a multi-institution team on a mixed-methods study of how physicists in Japan and the US are adopting AI.

Before that, I built a production RAG system on AWS Bedrock serving 1,000+ daily queries and multi-agent document workflows at Minty Living, and led the AI Trust Score framework for red-teaming enterprise LLMs at Tumeryk. I care most about LLM evaluation, grounding, and getting ML systems from notebook to production.

Want a quick tour? Click Ask about Yan in the corner to chat with Jeff, a citation-grounded RAG assistant I built from scratch to answer questions about my background.

I'm currently open to AI Engineer and Data Scientist roles. If you're working on challenging problems at the intersection of AI and engineering, I'd love to hear from you.
